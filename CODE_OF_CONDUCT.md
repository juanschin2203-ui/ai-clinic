# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Rocket Coding is a small, professional team building healthcare software. We expect contributors and team members to behave accordingly.

**Do:**
- Be respectful and constructive in code reviews and discussions
- Assume good intent; ask questions before assigning blame
- Credit others for their work
- Focus on the work, not the person
- Respect PHI and patient privacy — always

**Don't:**
- Harass, insult, or demean team members or contributors
- Share PHI, credentials, or confidential business info outside the team
- Use discriminatory language based on race, gender, religion, nationality, sexual orientation, disability, or any other protected characteristic
- Take credit for work that isn't yours

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to the product owner.

Reports are confidential. Retaliation against anyone reporting in good faith is prohibited.

## Consequences

Violations may result in warning, removal from the project, or termination of contract, depending on severity.
